TRANSPARENCY / PRIVACY

Simple by design.

This note explains what happens when you contact Gage or express interest in early testing. Last updated October 14, 2026.

Information you provide

If you request beta access, you may provide your name, email, role, CAD software, preferred beta-testing capability, and optional notes. We use this information to review interest in testing and contact you about the project or beta opportunities. We do not promise access or a launch date.

How form submissions work

Gage is a static site hosted by GitHub Pages. It does not run its own database. If the beta form has not been connected to a service, submitting it opens a prefilled email for you to review and send; no request is saved by Gage until you send the message. If the form is connected to Formspree, your details are sent to Formspree to process the request and make it available to the project owner. Formspree’s privacy practices also apply.

Preview screenshots and video

When available, reviewed screenshots and recordings are served as local website files. Videos do not autoplay or load third-party video embeds. A configured recording may load its poster, basic playback metadata, captions, and transcript when you visit the page; playback starts only when you choose it.

Analytics and cookies

This website does not intentionally install analytics trackers or advertising cookies. The site host and any enabled form provider may process technical information as part of delivering their services.

Sharing and retention

Contact details are used for project communication, not sold or rented. Form services and email providers may process information to deliver submissions. We retain interest requests as needed to manage testing and correspondence. Contact us to request removal of your submission or to stop receiving updates.

Your choices

You can ask what information you sent, request deletion, or withdraw permission for project updates by emailing andrew_mombay@brown.edu.

Independent project

Gage is an independent, unsigned MIT open-source preview and is not endorsed by OpenAI, Dassault Systèmes, or SOLIDWORKS. The app uses Codex usage. This marketing website has no AI account sign-in and does not ask for AI credentials. Advanced native qualification remains pending. This privacy note may change as the project and its hosting evolve.
