TRANSPARENCY / PRIVACY

Simple by design.

This note explains what happens when you contact Gage or express interest in early testing. Last updated October 18, 2026.

Information you provide

When you request a demo, early access or an answer to a question, we ask for your name, email address and request type. Company / employer, role, industry, CAD software, workflow interest and notes are optional. Notes may include preferred demo times and your time zone. We use these details to reply, arrange a possible demo, or assess your testing request. Consent covers contact about your request, not automatic newsletter enrollment. A demo request does not confirm a booking, and early access is not guaranteed. Please do not include confidential designs or sensitive personal information.

How form submissions work

Gage is a static site hosted by GitHub Pages. It does not run its own database. By default, the contact form prepares an email draft containing your answers. You must review and send it yourself; opening the draft does not deliver your request to Gage. Your email app or provider may retain the draft according to its own settings. If a Formspree endpoint is enabled, the form explains that your answers will be sent through Formspree to the project owner. Submission happens only when you press the send button. Formspree’s privacy practices also apply. This site has no external scheduling service, calendar booking or account sign-in. Form answers are not stored in cookies or browser local storage by this website.

Preview screenshots and video

When available, reviewed screenshots and recordings are served as local website files. Videos do not autoplay or load third-party video embeds. A configured recording may load its poster, basic playback metadata, captions, and transcript when you visit the page; playback starts only when you choose it.

Analytics and cookies

This website does not intentionally install analytics trackers or advertising cookies. The site host and any enabled form provider may process technical information as part of delivering their services.

Sharing and retention

Contact details are used for project communication, not sold or rented. Form services and email providers may process information to deliver submissions. We retain requests as needed to arrange demos, manage testing and respond to correspondence. Contact us to request removal of your submission or to stop receiving updates.

Your choices

You can ask what information you sent, request deletion, or withdraw permission for project updates by emailing andrew_mombay@brown.edu.

Independent project

Gage is an independent, unsigned MIT open-source preview and is not endorsed by OpenAI, Dassault Systèmes, or SOLIDWORKS. The app uses Codex usage. This marketing website has no AI account sign-in and does not ask for AI credentials. Advanced native qualification remains pending. This privacy note may change as the project and its hosting evolve.
