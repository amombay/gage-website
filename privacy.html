Privacy policy

How Gage uses the information you share when you get in touch. Updated November 16, 2026.

Information you provide

When you request a demo or ask a question, we ask for your name, email address and request type. Company / employer, role, industry, CAD software, workflow interest, and notes are optional. We use this information to respond to your request and arrange demos. Your consent covers a reply about your request, not automatic newsletter enrollment. A demo request does not confirm a booking. Please leave out confidential designs and sensitive personal information.

How we receive your request

GitHub Pages hosts this website. Preparing an email draft opens your selected Gmail or Outlook service with the information you entered. That provider may store the draft under its own privacy policy. Gage receives your request when you send the email. If a Formspree endpoint is configured, the form explains that it sends your details through Formspree when you submit. Formspree’s privacy practices also apply. This website has no account sign-in or external scheduling service.

Website media and cookies

We serve images and videos from the same site. The factory loop plays silently when your device allows motion. You can pause it using the player. This website uses no analytics trackers, advertising cookies, or browser storage for form answers. The hosting and email providers may process technical information when providing their services.

Retention and your choices

We keep contact details as needed to respond and arrange demos. We do not sell or rent contact information. To ask what information you provided or request removal, email andrew_mombay@brown.edu.

Independent project

Gage is an independent, open-source project. OpenAI and Dassault Systèmes do not endorse or sponsor Gage. This website does not ask for AI credentials.
