BEHIND THE PROJECT / 006

Built by engineers.
For engineers.

Gage started from a straightforward idea: engineers should spend more time solving design problems and less time repeating CAD commands. The project brings mechanical engineering experience together with AI tooling to explore a better workflow for creating parts, constructing assemblies, and preparing simulation studies.

Our north star is an engineer-led agent that does real CAD work in your design environment—without losing editability, traceability, or the ability to inspect and verify every result.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS / 008

Good questions.
Clear answers.

The practical details behind Gage, its capabilities, and our approach to AI integration.

What exactly is Gage?+

Gage is an independent AI CAD agent project built by engineers for engineers. Its aim is to translate natural-language design intent into real CAD operations—from parts and assemblies to supported simulation workflows.

Can Gage create CAD parts for me?+

Part creation and editing are core development priorities. The goal is for the agent to create sketches and parametric features, change dimensions, and build editable parts inside the connected CAD application. Exact commands and availability depend on the version being tested.

Can it build full assemblies?+

Assembly creation is a target workflow: bringing components together, placing them, applying compatible mates or constraints, and iterating on an assembled design. It is not being advertised as a generally available public feature yet.

Can Gage run simulations?+

Simulation orchestration is a development goal, including supported study setup, loading and boundary conditions, running a solver through compatible CAD software, and helping interpret outputs. Real solver access, licenses, study types, and engineering validation are required. Illustrative visuals are not actual simulation results.

Can I use my ChatGPT or Claude account?+

That's the intended experience where providers explicitly support account-connected third-party workflows. It's not a live Gage feature yet. Eligible ChatGPT plans may work with participating apps through official sign-in mechanisms. Anthropic generally recommends API authentication for independently built third-party products; a Claude subscription cannot be assumed to cover Gage usage. Depending on your provider and setup, you may need API access, separate credits, or compatible tooling. See ChatGPT account access guidance and Claude third-party authentication guidance.

Which CAD platforms will be supported?+

Gage is being developed with SOLIDWORKS-oriented workflows first. Broader CAD support is a possible future direction, but no additional compatibility has been announced.

Is Gage replacing the engineer?+

No. The goal is to offload repetitive modeling and study-setup tasks while keeping engineering decisions, reviews, and verification with a human. Generated geometry and simulation outputs must be checked before use.

How do I become a beta tester?+

Complete the early access request and share the CAD tools or workflows you're interested in. Gage is still in development; interest does not guarantee selection or a launch date.

Is Gage affiliated with OpenAI, Anthropic, or SOLIDWORKS?+

No. Gage is an independent project and is not sponsored by, endorsed by, or affiliated with OpenAI, Anthropic, Dassault Systèmes, or SOLIDWORKS. Product and brand names are used only to describe possible technical integrations.
